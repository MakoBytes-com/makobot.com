Always provide Russell a clickable link to whatever was just changed, built, or being discussed.

**Why:** Russell tests on the live site and needs to immediately see changes. Without a link he has to hunt for the page himself.

**How to apply:** After any deploy, commit, or UI change, include the live URL. When discussing a specific page, link it. Examples:
- "Done. See it at https://makobot.com/exchange"
- "Updated the nav: https://makobot.com/exchange/collections"
- "Admin moderation is at https://makobot.com/admin/exchange"

This applies to every project, not just makobot.com.
