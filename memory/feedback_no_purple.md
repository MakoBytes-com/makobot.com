Russell is not a fan of heavy purple in the UI. Small touches are fine, but it should never be a dominant or core color.

**Why:** Personal preference. Blue, grey, white, silver are his brand colors.

**How to apply:** Use blue (#3B82F6), grey (#8B95A8), white (#E8EDF3), and silver (#C0C8D8) for core UI (nav, buttons, borders, headings). Purple is OK sparingly — a platform badge, a small accent pill — but never for navigation, primary buttons, or large UI surfaces.
